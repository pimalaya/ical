## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: The timeline corpus is replayed

The walk of a series to instants SHALL be pinned by a committed corpus of the series on which recurring-ical-events answers alike, over the calendars of its own test suite, replayed by the test suite with no Python present. A second committed corpus SHALL pin each deliberate divergence with this crate's own answer and the reason for it. Since there is one oracle, no case SHALL enter either corpus without being read by a human.

#### Scenario: A regression in the timeline
- GIVEN a change that alters the occurrences of a series both implementations agreed on
- WHEN the test suite runs
- THEN the timeline replay fails, naming the calendar and the `UID`
