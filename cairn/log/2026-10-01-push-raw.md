# Raw lines restore byte for byte

`IcalCst::push_raw` (tree/cst.rs) appends raw logical lines, tokenised and kept byte for byte, as `VcardCst::push_raw` does for vCard. The Microsoft Graph event projection in io-msgraph restores the lines it stashed server-side through it rather than splicing them into the serialized text. A stashed subcomponent comes back as its lines, which serialize the same.

Capability moved: **decoded-model** (building a calendar from another representation).
